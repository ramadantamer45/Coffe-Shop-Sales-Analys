# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Power BI Project

## Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing coffee shop sales data. It is designed to help business owners, analysts, and stakeholders gain actionable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and operational trends. The dashboard leverages dynamic visualizations and advanced analytics to support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features
- **Comprehensive KPI Tracking:**
  - Total Sales, Total Orders, and Total Quantity Sold are tracked monthly, with automatic month-over-month (MoM) comparisons and difference calculations.
- **Dynamic Calendar Heat Map:**
  - Visualizes daily sales with color-coded intensity, allowing users to quickly spot high and low-performing days. Tooltips provide detailed metrics (Sales, Orders, Quantity) on hover.
- **Sales Segmentation:**
  - Breaks down sales by weekdays vs. weekends to reveal performance variations and patterns.
- **Store Location Analysis:**
  - Compares sales across different store locations, highlighting MoM changes to identify trends and opportunities.
- **Daily Sales Trend with Average Line:**
  - Displays daily sales for the selected month, including an average line to highlight exceptional days above or below the norm.
- **Product Category & Top Products:**
  - Analyzes sales by product category and showcases the top 10 products by sales volume for quick identification of best-sellers.
- **Hourly Sales Heat Map:**
  - Reveals sales patterns by day and hour, helping optimize staffing and promotions.

## Business Value
- **Actionable Insights:** Quickly identify sales trends, peak periods, and underperforming segments.
- **User-Friendly Interactivity:** Slicers and tooltips make it easy to explore data from multiple perspectives.
- **Performance Monitoring:** MoM metrics and visual cues highlight growth areas and potential issues.
- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Supports inventory, marketing, and operational strategies with clear, visual analytics.

## How to Use
1. Open the `Coffee Sales.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Use the slicers to select the desired month or filter by store location.
3. Hover over charts and heat maps for detailed tooltips and insights.
4. Explore each dashboard section for targeted analysis (sales trends, product performance, store comparisons, etc.).

## Project Files
- `Coffee Sales.pbix` – Power BI dashboard file
- `Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx` – Source data
- `MY SQL Queries.docx` – SQL queries used for data preparation
